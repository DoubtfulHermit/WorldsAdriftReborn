Who's watching

The watching number is how many browser tabs have this page open right now — not how many people, and not which ones. Two tabs count twice, and a tab you close fades out after about half a minute.

To count them at all, your browser makes up a random number when the page loads and sends it back with each update. Nothing else: no cookie, no account, no location, no record of where you came from. Reload and you're a new number. We keep a running total once a minute, and that total is all that's ever written down.
