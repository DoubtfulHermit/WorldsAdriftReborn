Operational layer

Persistent shared world

Operational

The community can authenticate, enter the world, meet other players, build ships and return to durable state.

5 / 5 represented objectives shipped

  • Native dedicated services — Linux game and login servers run continuously under systemd.
  • Accounts and character rosters — PostgreSQL-backed signup, login and persistent characters.
  • N-player co-presence — movement, appearance and basic equipment replicate between players.
  • Persistent construction — deployables, craft stations, ships, mounted parts and loose parts restore.
  • Server-authoritative scalar flight — helm, sails, mass, wind and durable hull state are live.
01

World layer

Island recovery

Expanding

Recovered geography and island systems are live in a bounded Tier-1 production rollout; the complete 254-island terrain rollout is not deployed.

4 / 5 represented objectives shipped

  • Real release topology — preserved WAMap identity and coordinates feed 47 live Tier-1 island domains.
  • Deterministic resources — biome-specific deposits, harvesting and per-player interest are authoritative.
  • Interest lifecycle — resources pace in, drain, unload and re-check out by viewer and region.
  • Understorm cycle — island lightning telegraphs and per-island resource refresh are deployed.
  • Complete terrain rollout — 254-island catalogue and distant shells exist, but are not deployed or visually accepted.
02

Authenticity layer

Retail flight reconstruction

In acceptance

The proven scalar flight remains authoritative while vector, collision and docking systems advance through shadow evidence before they are allowed to move a ship.

3 / 6 represented objectives shipped

  • Recovered world bounds — release-world push and clamp bands are live.
  • Residual drag and sail calibration — recovered settling behavior and measured sail balance are deployed.
  • Fixed clock and snapshots — Pack C restart and stall foundations passed their live run.
  • Vector force/torque shadow — observation is live; combined sail/turn smoothness still needs real eyes.
  • Engine and fuel lifecycle — server wiring and retail engine presentation are deployed; acceptance remains open.
  • Authority exit gate — vector motion, collision response, docking and damaging walls remain default-off work.
03

Simulation layer

Elastic domains

Local foundation

The boundaries needed for distribution are being made correct inside one process first. A domain is an ownership unit today—not a separate server.

3 / 6 represented objectives shipped

  • Stable region topology — islands map deterministically to canonical regions.
  • World directory — every registered entity is classified once as island, ship or explicit global scope.
  • Region-backed routing — resource interest consumes canonical ownership without changing its proven policy.
  • Local simulation domains — island and whole-ship ownership is deployed; full two-client acceptance remains incomplete.
  • Ship snapshot proof — pure capture/restore exists; destructive live proof and completeness audit remain.
  • Gateway seam — general command routing and forced handoff are not complete.
04

Infrastructure layer

Remote workers

Not live

This is the eventual multi-VPS system: the gateway keeps the legacy client connection while movable island or whole-ship domains run on other workers.

0 / 5 represented objectives shipped

  • Versioned worker messaging — domain commands and snapshots across a process boundary.
  • Experimental second worker — begin with an empty test island.
  • Single authority handoff — rotate generations and reject stale writes during movement.
  • Failure recovery — restore a committed snapshot without disconnecting the client.
  • Exit gate — no dual authority, no lost parts and no client-visible migration break.
05

Operations layer

Public access and observability

Operational

Players can join and stay current; operators can inspect the real world without exposing identity-bearing controls to the public map.

5 / 5 represented objectives shipped

  • Self-updating patcher — manifest, verified payloads and one player download path.
  • Public live map — anonymized geography and live world projection.
  • Account and crew portal — signup, login, password, alliance and emblem surfaces.
  • Authenticated World Inspector — linked world, simulation and infrastructure truth.
  • Public build log — regenerated from the repository's shipped history.

Elastic server, in plain language

One world endpoint.
Many possible homes.

The player should always connect to one familiar server. Behind it, Wareborn is preparing each island and entire ship as a unit that could eventually move to whichever VPS has room—without splitting a ship's hull, parts or aboard crew.

The hard rule

Only one authority at a time

Snapshot→Generation +1→New owner
Migration must reject stale writes, preserve the ship or island intact, recover after worker loss and keep the player connected.

Not live yet: remote workers, cross-VPS simulation messaging, authority migration, worker failover and distributed domain sleep. The current inspector reports these as unavailable rather than pretending they exist.
